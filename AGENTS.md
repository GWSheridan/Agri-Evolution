# Agri Evolution project requirements

- Use original Agri Evolution artwork. Do not import or reuse third-party mod artwork. Resizing or recolouring imported art does not make it original.
- Preserve user-approved artwork: v0.33.7 irrigation basins, seeds, farmland, machines, Genetic Clippers, Soil Sampler, journals, utility tools/bags, weeds, and all 39 crops approved through v0.37.0. Obtain user approval before replacing approved art.
- Finish existing Agri Evolution content before proposing unrelated features. Current selected backlog feature: irrigation sprinkler and valve; tiered crop supports remain pending.
- Work one feature at a time, retain backups/previous working JARs, and test new recipes against Minecraft 1.21.1 for conflicts.
- Keep asset provenance explicit. Excluded legacy source files are archival material, not approved assets for reuse. Do not claim all third-party textures are gone until the remaining asset audit is complete.
- Keep artwork masters, provenance records, and extraction scripts in the local `artwork/` directory. Historical art tools may require that archive. The game-ready assets live in `src/main/resources/`.
- Keep a single `CHANGELOG.md` with one section per released version. Add the new section at the top for each upload. Keep version-specific update notes and backups local.
- Keep historical asset reviews in local backups. Keep newly verified origin evidence with the affected assets.
