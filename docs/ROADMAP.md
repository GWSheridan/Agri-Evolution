# What's next for Agri Evolution

The next updates build on the crops and machines already in the mod. Resource harvests stay useful on their own, with optional processing and upgrades giving them more uses. Orchards are not part of this roadmap.

- **1.4 — Farm Planning:** a portable Farm Ledger for named fields, planting plans, seed counts, growing conditions and shared layouts.
- **1.5 — Better Growing Conditions:** rotation bonuses, compost mulch, more companion trials and clearer resource-crop catalyst advice through soil research.
- **1.6 — Resource Workshop:** one upgradeable machine for agricultural components. Early recipes support crop care and irrigation, middle recipes improve research and machinery, and late recipes use Nether and End harvests for advanced modules.
- **1.7 — Farm Production:** production targets, ingredient requests and links between resource processing, machines and cooking. More meals will use existing crops and vanilla meats.
- **1.8 — Connected Farms:** bulk produce storage, linked-machine supplies, shared production projects and farm achievement displays.

The later updates are planned work. Their recipes and balance will be worked out separately, with bonuses for good care rather than mandatory chores or replacement resource systems.
